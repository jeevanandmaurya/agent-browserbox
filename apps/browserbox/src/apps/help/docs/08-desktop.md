# Desktop and phone

The optional desktop (**Open desktop** in the chat list) shows BrowserBox's apps in windows. On phones and narrow windows it becomes a home screen instead. Both stay in step with the agent window: same files, same chats.

## Desktop

- Double-click icons to open apps; the files in `~/Desktop` appear there too. Drag files from your computer onto it.
- **Right-click** the desktop for New folder, New text file, Open Terminal here, sorting, wallpaper and settings; right-click icons, title bars and taskbar buttons for their own options.
- Drag a window to the left or right edge to fill half the screen, or to the top to maximise.
- The taskbar has the launcher (**Ctrl+K**), notifications, a calendar on the clock, and a show-desktop corner.
- The agent opens as a window; right-click it for **Open in new tab**.

## Phone

- The status bar opens quick settings and notifications.
- The home screen has a clock, your apps, search and a dock.
- The bar at the bottom: tap or swipe up to go home; long-press or swipe up and hold for recent apps (tap a card to switch, swipe it up to close).
- The back button goes home.

## Themes and wallpaper

Settings → Appearance → **Theme** colours everything at once: the agent window, the desktop, terminal colours and the wallpaper. Graphite, Midnight, Forest, Ember, Frost, Petal and Ink are dark; Paper and Dune are light; **Match device** follows your device's light or dark setting.

Each theme has its own wallpaper, and every wallpaper style can be drawn in any theme's colours: mountain ridges, topographic contours, dunes, a Bauhaus poster, a retro sunset, a pixel skyline and a starfield. They are drawn by the browser at your screen's size, so they stay sharp and download nothing. Or choose your own picture.
